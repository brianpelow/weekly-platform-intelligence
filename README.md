# weekly-platform-intelligence

> A weekly executive brief on platform engineering, agentic AI, and regulated industry technology -- published every Monday by an autonomous agent.

![Weekly Brief](https://github.com/brianpelow/weekly-platform-intelligence/actions/workflows/weekly.yml/badge.svg)

## What it is

Every Monday at 09:00 UTC, an autonomous agent:

1. Gathers signals from RSS feeds, HackerNews, GitHub trending, and regulatory agency sites
2. Scores items for relevance to platform engineering and regulated industries
3. Synthesizes a 400-word executive brief using an LLM
4. Publishes it as a GitHub Discussion
5. Archives it to this repo

**Follow this repo** to receive the brief in your GitHub notifications every Monday.

## Who it is for

Engineering leaders in regulated financial services and manufacturing who care about:

- Platform engineering and developer productivity
- Agentic AI and autonomous systems
- Compliance automation and AI governance
- DORA metrics, SRE, and operational excellence
- Regulatory developments: OCC, FCA, ECB, FFIEC, EU AI Act
